Emergency Protocols

Comprehensive Immediate Action Guidelines

🫀 Heart Attack (Cardiac Arrest)

  • Call emergency services immediately.
  • If the person is unconscious, begin CPR (Push hard and fast in the center of the chest).
  • Loosen any tight clothing.
  • If trained, use an AED (Automated External Defibrillator) if available.

🔥 Severe Burns

  • Cool the burn under cold running water for at least 10 minutes.
  • Do NOT apply ice, butter, or ointments immediately.
  • Cover the burn loosely with a sterile, non-fluffy dressing or cling film.
  • Seek medical help if the burn is large or deep.

🩸 Severe Bleeding

  • Apply direct pressure to the wound using a clean cloth or bandage.
  • Maintain pressure until bleeding stops.
  • Elevate the injured area above the heart if possible.
  • Do not remove the cloth; if it soaks through, add another layer on top.

😵 Choking (Adult)

  • Stand behind the person and wrap your arms around their waist.
  • Give 5 sharp back blows between the shoulder blades.
  • If unsuccessful, perform 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver).
  • Alternate until the blockage is dislodged.

🐍 Snake Bite

  • Keep the patient calm and completely still to slow venom spread.
  • Keep the bitten limb at or slightly below heart level.
  • Do NOT cut the wound, suck out venom, or apply a tight tourniquet.
  • Rush to the nearest hospital for anti-venom immediately.

⚡ Electric Shock

  • Do NOT touch the person if they are still in contact with the source.
  • Turn off the main power supply immediately if possible.
  • If you cannot turn it off, use a dry non-conducting object (like wood) to push them away.
  • Check for breathing and start CPR if necessary.

🧠 Seizures (Epilepsy)

  • Clear the area of hard or sharp objects to prevent injury.
  • Cushion their head with a soft jacket or pillow.
  • Do NOT hold them down or put anything in their mouth.
  • Once the seizure stops, roll them onto their side (recovery position).

🦴 Bone Fractures

  • Do not try to realign the bone or push a bone that's sticking out back in.
  • Immobilize the injured area using a splint or sling if possible.
  • Apply an ice pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling.
  • Keep the patient still and seek immediate medical attention.

☀️ Heatstroke

  • Move the person to a cool, shaded, or air-conditioned place.
  • Remove excess clothing and cool them down with damp cloths or cool water.
  • Fan the person vigorously to lower body temperature.
  • If conscious, give them cool water to sip slowly.

⚠️ Stroke (F.A.S.T. Method)

  • Face: Ask them to smile. Does one side of the face droop?
  • Arms: Ask them to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?
  • Speech: Ask them to repeat a simple phrase. Is their speech slurred?
  • Time: If you observe any of these signs, call emergency immediately.

🫁 Asthma Attack

  • Sit the person upright comfortably and keep them calm.
  • Help them use their reliever inhaler (usually blue) - 1 puff every 30-60 seconds (up to 10 puffs).
  • If symptoms don't improve after 10 puffs, call an ambulance.
  • Do not lay them down flat.

🧪 Poisoning

  • Identify what the person swallowed, inhaled, or was exposed to.
  • Call emergency or poison control immediately.
  • Do NOT force the person to vomit unless instructed by a medical professional.
  • If unconscious but breathing, place them in the recovery position.
